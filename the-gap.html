The Gap

How humans lived, against how they live now.

Each record below takes one condition of human life, such as how many people a person knows or who helps raise a baby. It says what that condition was in the world humans evolved in, what it is today, and how far apart the two are. Researchers call the world humans evolved in the EEA, the environment of evolutionary adaptedness. Every record carries a label that says how well its ancestral picture was tested.
